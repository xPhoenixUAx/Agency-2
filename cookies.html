About cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small values saved by your browser and sent with requests to the relevant website. Session storage keeps information in the current browser tab. We use these technologies to protect the audit form, remember your privacy choice and, with your permission, carry navigation information between pages.

This policy applies to the website operated by Your registered company name, trading as SIGNAL. For our company details and how we handle personal information, see the Privacy Policy.

Storage used on this website

The website currently uses the following first-party storage. It does not currently use advertising pixels, audience analytics cookies or embedded advertising-platform widgets.

Form security and server records

The form cookie is marked HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it, and SameSite=Strict to restrict cross-site use. It is marked Secure when served over HTTPS.

The server also uses a hashed IP-based record to limit repeated form requests. This record is stored on the server, not in a cookie. Website hosting may generate access and error logs. See the Privacy Policy for retention information.

Your choices

On your first visit, choose “Essential only”, “Allow all” or open the settings to choose navigation storage separately. Optional navigation storage stays off until you allow it. Use in the footer or here to change your choice at any time. Turning navigation storage off removes its existing values.

Your choice is remembered for 180 days on this browser and website. If your browser blocks saving preferences, the choice applies only to the current page and the banner will appear again on another page or visit.

You can delete cookies and site storage or block cookies in your browser’s privacy settings. Blocking the form cookie may prevent an audit request from being submitted. You can still browse the public pages or contact us by email.

If navigation storage is declined or unavailable, links remain usable and transitions may be less smooth. Your device’s reduced-motion setting disables navigation animation.

There are currently no advertising or audience analytics cookies to opt into on this website. If we introduce technologies that require consent, we will provide the required choice before activating them. The privacy acknowledgement in the audit form does not give consent to advertising cookies.

Third-party websites

Following an external link takes you to a separate website. That site may set its own cookies and use other technologies under its own policies. Mentioning Google Ads, YouTube or Analytics on our service pages does not itself activate those services’ tracking on this website.
